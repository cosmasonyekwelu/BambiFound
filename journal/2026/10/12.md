### Core Thesis
This course covers the agency and sales layer rather than technical development. Building chatbots is common, but the real difficulty lies in finding buyers, positioning the offer, pricing correctly, and managing client delivery after closing.

The core model is an AI staffing agency. Instead of selling a software subscription, I place one AI employee into a single vertical industry and price it against the target role's salary.

### Proof and Traction
In his third month, Mani made $200 from a chatbot and almost quit. A $300 consulting call subsequently turned into a $132,000 deal closed in 38 days. He broke down his last three deals and the exact strategy used.

### Chapter Breakdown
* **Chapter 1: Vertical Selection (0:25):** Focus on one industry using four weighted tests, the Harvey scorecard, and a structured plan for the first 30 days.
* **Chapter 2: Insights and Failure Scenarios (1:05):** Identify 15 failure modes specific to LLM deployments in that vertical.
* **Chapter 3: The Offer (1:29):** Staff entire departments instead of selling chatbots. Balance pain, price, and proof while competing against payroll costs. Progress clients through a ladder from pilot to roster to full department.
* **Chapter 4: The Prototype (2:25):** Closed four deals without using a pitch deck. Prioritize design first, contrasting Emergent versus Lovable approaches.
* **Chapter 5: Funnel and Nurture (3:04):** Map top, middle, and bottom funnel content. Use lead magnets, calendar booking friction as a filter, and email nurture series.
* **Chapter 6: Pipeline Channels A through J (3:56):** Work through warm network, live events, online communities, YouTube, LinkedIn content, LinkedIn outbound, cold email, Upwork, systematic follow-up, Dream 100 targets, and physical outreach. Standardize execution using CVOS (consistency, volume, optimisation, scaling).
* **Chapter 7: Discovery and Audit (9:16):** Give the AI employee persistent memory. Conduct a 30-minute role audit using real client financial metrics.
* **Chapter 8: Proposal (10:02):** Select the target roles for the client. Ensure any price discount trades for tangible value.
* **Chapter 9: Contract (10:30):** Convert the proposal directly into the contract agreement. Include a specialized clause for first-time AI buyers.
* **Chapter 10: Onboarding (10:56):** Access permissions are where projects stall. Use the onboarding questionnaire as the technical build specification.
* **Chapters 11 through 14 (11:16+):** Manage delivery cadence, build protocol, testing loops, client feedback, retention strategies, and upsells.

### Operating Rules
* Describe what the AI employee does, not the underlying framework or stack.
* Write sales copy in my own natural voice so the AI copies my writing style.
* Never give an unreciprocated discount. Require a case study, testimonial, or longer term commitment in exchange.
* Give value first in online communities and establish rapport with community admins.
* Inspect every prospect manually. Deliver upfront value before making an ask.
