# AI Staffing Agency Playbook

Commercial framework for placing AI employees into specific industry verticals.

## Core Value Proposition
Selling software tools or chatbots creates pricing pressure and high churn. The AI staffing agency model positions AI deployments as digital workers that replace or augment human headcount.

* **Anchor to Payroll:** Price against the salary of the target role rather than software subscription benchmarks.
* **Vertical Focus:** Select a single industry niche to standardize domain knowledge, compliance needs, and workflows.
* **Department Ladder:** Start with a targeted pilot role, expand to a team roster, and scale into full departmental coverage.

## Vertical Selection and Audit
1. **Four Weighted Tests:** Evaluate industry margin, pain severity, workflow standardization, and technical accessibility.
2. **Harvey Scorecard:** Score potential verticals to identify low-complexity, high-value entry points.
3. **Failure Mapping:** Document 15 vertical-specific failure scenarios to prevent technical and operational bottlenecks.
4. **First 30 Days Execution:** Validate industry demand before building complex custom tools.

## Sales Pipeline and Outbound Channels (CVOS)
Build outbound momentum using the CVOS framework: Consistency, Volume, Optimisation, and Scaling.

* **Warm Network and Events:** Map existing contacts and attend industry conferences.
* **Communities and Admin Partnerships:** Share useful insights in niche groups and build direct relationships with community leaders.
* **Content Engine:** Publish YouTube breakdowns and structured LinkedIn posts.
* **Outbound Outreach:** Combine targeted LinkedIn outreach, cold emails, Upwork proposals, physical mailers, and a Dream 100 list.
* **Manual Verification:** Review each prospect manually to ensure pitch relevancy before reaching out.

## Discovery, Proposal, and Contracting
* **30-Minute Role Audit:** Analyze target job descriptions, salary costs, manual hours, and software integrations using real operational numbers.
* **Memory and Context:** Provision persistent memory so the AI employee retains long-term operational context across interactions.
* **Proposal Structure:** Select the exact roles for the client instead of offering an open ended scope.
* **Discount Rule:** Require an exchanged asset for any fee reduction, such as a video testimonial, documented case study, or extended contract term.
* **Agreement Clause:** Convert the approved proposal into the final contract. Include a explicit clause designed for first-time AI buyers.

## Onboarding and Account Delivery
* **Onboarding Questionnaire:** Treat the client onboarding form as the primary technical specification document.
* **Access Protocols:** Secure system access early, as credential bottlenecks are the primary reason projects stall.
* **Delivery Cadence:** Establish clear feedback loops, testing schedules, and retention upsell milestones.
