## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a serene, high-conviction ecosystem tailored for visionary founders, tier-one technical operators, and venture builders. Blending organic editorial poise with disciplined modern SaaS architecture, the visual style expresses a refined "forest & compass" philosophy: deep pine tones anchor authority, warm linen grounds the environment in human craft, and radiant amber accents indicate algorithmic precision and direction.

### Design Principles
- **Quiet Clarity:** Eradicate ambient clutter. Every container, border, and badge serves an orienting purpose, replacing hyper-stimulating SaaS tropes with calm, measured confidence.
- **Natural Tactility:** Digital interfaces must feel grounded. By pairing warm linen foundations with delicate, tactile card elevations, interfaces project the stability of classic print artifacts elevated by intelligent software.
- **Humanized Intelligence:** AI capabilities are not framed as mystical or synthetic; instead, they function as ambient wayfinding cues—directional compass bearings that guide founders and talent directly toward high-value synergies.

## Colors

The palette leverages an organic, low-strain tonal architecture engineered for prolonged strategic workflows and profile deep dives.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#183B2B`):** Deep Forest Pine. Used for high-emphasis interactive surfaces, active state anchors, and primary CTA buttons. In high-contrast pairings, use Deep Pine Shade (`#11281D`) for pressed or focused states.
- **Secondary (`#E7EFEA`):** Soft Sage Surface. Used for subtle chip fills, active navigation rails, pill highlights, and light-tinted metric containers. Supported by Sage Tint (`#D6E3D9`) for structural hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#D97736`):** Compass Warm Amber. Represents algorithmic relevance, match scoring, directional milestones, and curated highlights. Supported by Deep Amber (`#C26322`) for interactive links and icon accents.
- **Neutral Base & Canvas:**
  - Base Background: Off-White Warm Linen (`#FBFBF9`).
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Canvas: Muted Linen (`#F4F4F0`).
  - Structural Divider & Ghost Border: Refined Slate Green (`#E4E7E3`).
  - Primary Typography: Deep Carbon Slate (`#191C1A`).
  - Secondary Typography: Soft Charcoal Pine (`#3A3F3B`).
  - Muted Editorial Text: Subtle Stone (`#6E746F`).
- **Semantic / AI Accents:**
  - Emerald Signal (`#1E824C` text on `#E8F5E9` background): High-probability match states, verified credentials, and venture status.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Hanken Grotesk** across display, reading, and interface layers. Its architectural geometry, open counters, and humanist proportions deliver crisp readability inside data-dense dashboard modules while projecting editorial distinction at larger display scales.

### Typesetting Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered with tight letter tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to create clean visual cohesion without appearing condensed.
- **Numeric Tabulation:** When showing compatibility percentages, funding figures, or headcount stats, activate tabular lining numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict alignment.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Small tags and badges make deliberate use of medium-to-semibold weights with neutral-to-expanded tracking to safeguard legibility against soft tinted backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model balances generous negative space with structured density, keeping strategic overviews uncluttered.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a maximum content shell of `1360px`.
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem` (`24px`), `margin: 2.5rem` (`40px`).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem` (`20px`), `margin: 2rem` (`32px`).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm: 1rem` (`16px`), `margin-sm: 1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Internal Rhythm:** Component padding operates in strict increments of `8px` and `16px`. Standard cards utilize `1.5rem` (`24px`) internal padding on desktop and collapse to `1rem` (`16px`) on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered planar approach rather than high-contrast structural drops, maintaining an organic, tactile feel.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **L0 - Canvas Root (`#FBFBF9`):** The primary warm foundation for application shell layouts and page roots.
2. **L1 - Surface Container (`#FFFFFF` or `#F4F4F0`):** Standard state for interactive and structural cards. Borders use the refined slate tint `#E4E7E3` (`1px solid`). Shadows are faint, ambient, and warm-toned:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(25, 28, 26, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(25, 28, 26, 0.02)`.
3. **L2 - Interactive Hover & Popover:** Triggered on card focus or hovering over talent profiles:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.07)`.
   The border shifts to `#D6E3D9` with a subtle transform lift of `-1px`.
4. **L3 - Modals & Critical Overlays:**
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(25, 28, 26, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(25, 28, 26, 0.04)`.
   Paired with a low-opacity warm backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(6px); background-color: rgba(25, 28, 26, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored organic radius hierarchy, leaning into friendly contours that maintain architectural rigor.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `0.875rem` (`14px`) to `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius to create inviting content envelopes without geometric distortion.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (`8px`) to maintain structural rhythm when arranged in horizontal rows.
- **Pills, Badges & AI Status Indicators:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) to emphasize continuous, fluid status signals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Pine background (`#183B2B`), crisp linen text (`#FBFBF9`), `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius, semibold label. On hover, background shifts to `#11281D`. Active state applies a scale transformation of `0.985`.
- **Secondary:** Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E4E7E3` border and dark charcoal text (`#191C1A`). On hover, transition background to `#E7EFEA` and border to `#D6E3D9`.
- **Compass Accent:** Amber primary (`#D97736`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), strictly reserved for high-intent AI generation, synergy confirmations, or "Request Intro" actions. Hover shifts to `#C26322`.

### Chips & AI Signal Badges
- **AI Match Indicator:** Pill geometry with soft Sage background (`#E7EFEA`), Deep Pine text (`#183B2B`), and a leading illuminated Compass Amber pip (`#D97736`). Example: `AI Match 94%`.
- **Complementary Skill Tags:** Light linen fill (`#F4F4F0`), refined border (`#E4E7E3`), slate charcoal text (`#3A3F3B`). Hover shifts background to `#E7EFEA`.
- **Venture Status Tags:** Light emerald background (`#E8F5E9`), emerald text (`#1E824C`), semibold tracking.

### Cards & Profiles
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E4E7E3`, radius of `1rem` (`16px`), padding `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- Header displays entity avatar, verified tick, name, and top-right AI relevance indicator. Dividers within cards are hairline (`1px solid #F4F4F0`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Surface in Linen (`#FFFFFF` on `#FBFBF9` canvas), border `1px solid #E4E7E3`, height `44px`, radius `0.5rem` (`8px`), text `#191C1A`.
- Placeholder text in soft stone (`#6E746F`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#183B2B` with a `2px` concentric glow in `rgba(24, 59, 43, 0.12)`. No harsh browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Box/circle outline: `1.5px solid #D6E3D9`, rounded `4px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio).
- Checked state: `#183B2B` background with a crisp white checkmark icon or inner dot, animating in smoothly via ease-out transition.

### Lists & Synergy Feeds
- Row items use transparent backgrounds with a subtle bottom divider (`1px solid #E4E7E3`).
- On row hover, smooth transition to a soft Sage-tinted background (`#F4F7F5`) with a `0.5rem` (`8px`) inner radius padding shift for clear interaction feedback.
