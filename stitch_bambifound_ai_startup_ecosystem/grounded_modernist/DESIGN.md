## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, humanistic editorial aesthetic tailored for high-conviction collaborative ventures. It blends the grounding reliability of classic institutions with the crisp utility of modern venture tooling. The visual tone is mature, thoughtful, and organic—evoking architectural craftsmanship, archival paper, and deep foliage rather than hyper-digitized tech paradigms.

### Emotional Disposition
- **Steadfast & Trustworthy:** Conveys patient capital, structural longevity, and professional safety through disciplined typography and deep organic tones.
- **Warmly Intelligent:** Replaces cold algorithmic neutrality with tactile warmth, cream surfaces, and restrained earthen accents.
- **Intentional & Focused:** Emphasizes high content density without clutter through strict spatial grouping, hairline separations, and generous breathing room.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in deep botanical greens and warm mineral creams, punctuated by a controlled terracotta accent for focal intent.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#14281D`):** The foundational anchor. Used for primary interactive triggers, dominant editorial headlines, and active focus boundaries. Deep forest tint prevents sterile contrast against warm backdrops.
- **Primary Container (`#1B3828`):** Elevated brand elements, emphasized cards, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Secondary (`#2D5A40` & `#3E7B57`):** Structural supporting green, icon accents, secondary active states, and hovered indicators.
- **Sage Tint (`#E8F0EB`):** Low-contrast pill backgrounds, informational tags, and subtle affirmative states.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C86D44`):** Warm terracotta reserved exclusively for high-salience moments: match indicators, verified founder seals, and primary callout pips. Used sparingly to preserve weight.
- **Surface & Canvas:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Alabaster)
  - Card / Surface Elevate: `#FFFFFF` and `#F4EFE6` (Linen White / Warm Cream)
  - Structural Hairlines: `#EAE3D6` and `#E5DFD5`
- **Text & Ink Hierarchy:**
  - Ink Primary: `#1C201D` (Deep carbon-moss)
  - Ink Secondary: `#4A554D` (Weathered stone)
  - Ink Muted: `#7A877E` (Field lichen)

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a deliberate dual-font architecture:
1. **Newsreader** handles editorial prestige, hero titles, and high-impact section markers, injecting artisanal authority and personal warmth.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers pragmatic legibility across body text, tabular displays, labels, and micro-copy.

### Typesetting Rules
- Display headlines use standard sentence casing with negative tracking to eliminate loose serifs.
- Small labels and data tags leverage gentle positive letter-spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to ensure quick recognition in metadata-dense views.
- Line heights in body copy maintain an open 1.55–1.6 ratio to complement the cream canvas and improve long-form reading stamina.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an asymmetric 12-column grid anchored by generous outer margins, balancing density in profile matching cards with editorial pacing.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, max-width 1280px, 24px gutters, and 32px canvas margins. Structural dashboards can stretch to 1440px with sticky contextual rail sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 24px margins. Sub-panes condense into layered off-canvas sheets.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 16px margins. Horizontal scrolling rail cards transition to clean vertical stacks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal stratification** and **ambient earthen diffusion**, bypassing stark black drop shadows or heavy industrial borders.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base viewport finished in `#FBF9F5`. Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFE6` bounded by a 1px solid hairline (`#EAE3D6`). Shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(20, 40, 29, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(20, 40, 29, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Cards):** Scaled lift with soft tinted ambient bloom: `0px 12px 28px -6px rgba(20, 40, 29, 0.07), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(20, 40, 29, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` resting over a tinted scrim (`rgba(20, 40, 29, 0.40)` with a 3px blur). Shadow: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 40, 29, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft, tailored curvature with geometric discipline.
- **Interactive Triggers (Buttons, Chips, Filter Tags):** Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full` or 9999px) for ergonomic appeal and tactile distinction.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, Modals, Feed Boxes):** `16px` (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`), evoking archival stationary and bound portfolios.
- **Form Inputs & Dropdowns:** `10px` to `12px` to sit comfortably between rectangular structure and pill-shaped actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#14281D`, text `#FBF9F5`, fully rounded. Hover transitions to `#1B3828` with an elevation lift of -1px. Active state compresses gently (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#EAE3D6`, text `#1C201D`. Hover shifts to `#F4EFE6` with border shifting to `#D4CBBF`.
- **Accent:** Terracotta `#C86D44` surface with white text, dedicated solely to top-priority matchmaking conversions.

### Chips & Badges
- **Skill / Role Chip:** Pill-shaped, `#E8F0EB` fill, `#14281D` text. On selection, shifts to `#14281D` fill with `#FBF9F5` text and an inline animated checkmark.
- **Match Indicator Badge:** `#F4EFE6` pill with `#C86D44` dot beacon and bold percentage indicator.

### Input Fields
- Solid `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E5DFD5` border, 12px vertical padding. Focus creates an inward 1px forest green border (`#14281D`) accompanied by a subtle ambient outer halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 90, 64, 0.12)`.
- Persistent, concise labels set in `label-sm` with charcoal `#4A554D`.

### Cards (Builder Profile & Match Cards)
- Grounded in `#FFFFFF`, edged with a 1px `#EAE3D6` boundary. Features generous inner padding (`space-lg`), integrated skill tag rows, and an anchored bottom utility bar separating conversational CTA triggers from metadata tags.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hairline borders in `#7A877E`. Selected state fills with `#14281D` with crisp off-white glyphs.
